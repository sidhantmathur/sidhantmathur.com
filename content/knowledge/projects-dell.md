# Propensity model at Dell

A cross-sell propensity model, built as an intern. Sidhant's role: marketing
intern at Dell Technologies in Round Rock, TX, May 2018 – Aug 2018.

He built a propensity model in Azure ML that found about 20,000 cross-sell
accounts. At the segment's average deal size, those accounts are worth about
$129M. That figure is a sizing (accounts times average deal size), not
pipeline. He then trained the sales and marketing teams to use the model's
output for lead prioritization.
