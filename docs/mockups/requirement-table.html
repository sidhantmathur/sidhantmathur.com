What changes, beyond looks

  • The soft-pedal has nowhere to hide. Left, "that's a quick ramp" dissolves the Looker gap mid-sentence — the exact phrasing the Stage 0 eval flagged. Right, it's a row tagged unmet; the prose can still soften it, but the tag is already read.
  • "Unclear" stops becoming a false "met." Nothing in the corpus covers people management. Today that requirement silently vanishes. The checklist has a slot for "the posting asks something the corpus can't answer," which is the honest answer.
  • Citations become enforceable per row — a row that can't cite gets downgraded to unclear by code, not by the model's judgment. That's idea #21, and the checklist is the shape that makes it visible.
  • Counts are skimmable in about a second — 4 met · 2 partial · 1 unmet · 1 unclear is the six-second-scan answer that a prose block isn't.
  • Cost: rows resolve one at a time as they stream, which reads better than a block appearing at once. But it's a bigger schema, a longer generation, and it makes the assessment look more precise than it is — a tag reads as a measurement.
