## Notes on factual accuracy

  real colleague. The resume says "one finance analyst's entire workday, every
  day of quarter-end close" — no gender, and a workday rather than a week.
- "Business degree, self-taught engineer" (Haiku 12) calls him an engineer,
  which is the framing the whole rewrite is trying to move away from.
- "Built right the first time" (Haiku 7) is not a claim the resume supports.
